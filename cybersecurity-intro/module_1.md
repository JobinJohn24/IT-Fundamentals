### Terminology

- `C.I.A` - confidentiality (read only by the right people), integrity (only be changed by authorized personnal), availability (available to read & use whenever)
- case study:
  - Equifax:
  - reported data breach of 147 million people.
  - the exposed data contained names, DOB, SS, address & other personal information.
  - because of a security certificate that wasn't renewed, the monitoring of network traffic hadn't been looked after for 19 months = more vulnerable to attacks.
  - since there was a backdoor in the apache struts software (which holds the ACIS database), they were warned but failed to install the software updates.
  - this reported a data breach that cost Equifax $1,400,000,000.
  - then settled with the FTC for $575,000,000 & a free monitoring & identify theft service up to 10 years.

### Malware

- terms associated with software that attempts to harm computers in different ways.
  1. ransomware - demanding payment to refrain from doing harmful actions.
  2. spyware - recording of the users activites, passwords, and transmits the information.
  3. botnets - malware that allows the attacker to control a computer group for attacks
     1. sending spam emails
     2. flooding websites with request (denial-of-service attack)
