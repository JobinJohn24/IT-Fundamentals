### AI-assisted research workflow

- AI doesn't replace critical thinking, just eliminates retrrieval & summarization steps you spend time on insight.
- old workflow: `Google` → scan 10 tabs → summarize manually → cite sources (2 hours)
- new workflow: `Perplexity AI query` → read cited synthesis → `prompt Claude to expand with analysis` → cross-check via Google Scholar (20 minutes)

### Writing with AI: augmentation, *NOT* replacement

- It's a collaborative, not delegated.
- Person 1 provides the structure, key claims, and authentic voice.
- AI: drafts, expands and polishes.
- Helps with writer's block.
