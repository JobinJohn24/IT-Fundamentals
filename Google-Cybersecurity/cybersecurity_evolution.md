# Cybersecurity Evolution

### 8 CISSP Security Domains
- `Security and Risk Management`
  - security goals, objectives, risk mitigation, compliance, and business continuity
- `Assest security`
  - storage, maintenance, & destruction of data
- `security architecture and engineering`
  - optimization of security using tools, systems, and processes
- `communications and networks security`
  - physical network and wireless communications management
- `identity and access management`
  - keeping data secure through policies
- `security assessment and testing`
  - conducting security control testing, analyzing data, and security audits for risks, threats, and vulnerabilities
- `security operations`
  - investigations and implementation of preventative measures
- `software development security`
  - secure coding practices, guidelines used to create secure apps and services
