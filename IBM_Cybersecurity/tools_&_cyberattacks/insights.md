### WarGames Impact on CS

- released during 1983
- loosely based on WW2
- first CS policy

- synopsis
  - student (David) sneaks onto a military supercomputer and initiates what he believed to be a simulation
  - he initiated a thermonuclear war (Global Thermonuclear War)
  - WOPR - responsible for predicting nuclear war outcomes
  - he prompted the countdown which initiated a:
    - national panic
    - accusation of national espionage
  - the inspiration was built on tic-tac-toe, but the winning move was to not play
    - halting countdown
  - concern:
    - Ronald Regan worried about security suspectibilty
    - developed a National Cybersecurity Policy
      - first national telecommunication policy
      - NSDD 145
        - Directive to safeguard the following:
          - national communciation
          - information systems
          - government capabilities
          - optimizing the allocation of government resources
          - mandating the protection of all systems in generating, storing, processing, transferring, or communicating classified information
          - encourage the private sector to handle private information
          - developing protective strategies on exploitation and risks 
      - national security:
        - established the NTISSC
        - developed establishment policies
