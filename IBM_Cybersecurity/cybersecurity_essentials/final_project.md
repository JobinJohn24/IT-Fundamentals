# Final Project

### Case Study
- Yahoo! data breach
- affecting 3 billion user accounts
- hackers gained unauthorized access to the internal systems & sensitive information

- leaked or lost data
  - personal information
    - names, email addresses, numbers, DOBs, and hashed passwords

- impact
  - it was used for identity theft, spamming, phishing attacks and malicious activities

- prevention techniques
  - stronger passwords
  - MFA
  - security audits
  - employee training
  - encryption & access controls

### Case study
- Equifax data breach
- The breach exposed personal and financial information for 147 million people.

- leaked or lost data
  - large amounts of PII
  - social security numbers, used for identity theft and fradulent activities

- impact
  - at risk of:
    - identity theft
    - financial fraud
    - malicious activity
  - multiple lawsuits
  - regulatory investigations
  - stock value decline

- prevention techniques
  - regular security audits
  - MFA 
  - improved network segmentation & access controls
  - sensitive data encryption
  - employee training and awareness programs
  - software patching for vulnerabilities
